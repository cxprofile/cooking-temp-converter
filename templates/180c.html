180°C to Fahrenheit (Oven Temperature Guide)

180°C = 350°F

180°C is considered a moderate oven temperature and is one of the most common settings in baking.

What is 180°C used for?

  • Cakes 🍰
  • Cookies 🍪
  • Muffins
  • Roasting vegetables 🥕
