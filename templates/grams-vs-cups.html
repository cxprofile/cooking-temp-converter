Grams vs Cups - Which Is Better?

When to use Grams vs Cups in Cooking

Grams and cups are two of the most common units used in cooking, but they measure very different things. Grams measure weight, while cups measure volume. This difference can lead to inconsistencies, especially when measuring ingredients like flour or sugar.

Cups measure volume, while grams measure weight — and this difference can significantly affect your cooking results.

For example, 1 cup of flour can vary depending on how it’s scooped, but 120 grams of flour will always be the same. This is why many professional bakers prefer using grams for better accuracy and consistency.

Using cups can be convenient, but it often depends on how the ingredient is packed. A cup of flour can vary significantly depending on whether it is scooped, spooned, or sifted.

Grams, on the other hand, provide a precise measurement every time. This makes them the preferred choice for professional bakers and anyone who wants consistent results.

For best results, use grams when accuracy matters, and cups when convenience is more important. When converting between the two, it’s helpful to use a reliable conversion tool to avoid errors.
