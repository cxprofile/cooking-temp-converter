What is 180°C Used For?

An oven temperature of 180°C (356°F) is one of the most commonly used settings in cooking and baking. It provides even heat that is ideal for a wide range of recipes, especially baked goods and roasted dishes.

Cakes 🍰

180°C is perfect for baking soft and fluffy cakes. This temperature allows the batter to rise evenly without burning the outside too quickly.

  • Best for sponge cakes and butter cakes
  • Ensures even baking and good texture
  • Typical baking time: 25-35 minutes

Cookies 🍪

Cookies bake well at 180°C, giving them a slightly crisp edge while keeping the center soft.

  • Great for chocolate chip and sugar cookies
  • Helps achieve golden-brown edges
  • Typical baking time: 10-15 minutes

Muffins

Muffins benefit from consistent heat, and 180°C helps them rise nicely while staying moist inside.

  • Ideal for blueberry, banana, and chocolate muffins
  • Promotes even rising and soft texture
  • Typical baking time: 18-25 minutes

Roasting Vegetables 🥕

Roasting vegetables at 180°C brings out natural sweetness while keeping them tender inside.

  • Works well for carrots, potatoes, and broccoli
  • Helps achieve light caramelization
  • Typical cooking time: 25-40 minutes
