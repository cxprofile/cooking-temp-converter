When to use Grams vs Ounces

When to use Grams vs Ounces in Cooking

When cooking or baking, choosing between grams and ounces can make a big difference in the accuracy of your recipe. While both units measure weight, they are used differently depending on the type of recipe and where it comes from.

Grams are part of the metric system and are commonly used in most countries around the world. They provide precise measurements, which is especially important in baking. Recipes that use grams tend to be more accurate because they measure exact weight rather than volume.

Ounces, on the other hand, are commonly used in the United States. They are convenient for quick measurements but may not always be as precise as grams. Some recipes also use fluid ounces, which measure volume instead of weight, adding to the confusion.

In general, grams are best used for baking and recipes that require precision, such as cakes, bread, and pastries. Ounces are more commonly used for cooking, where exact measurements are less critical.

If you’re following an international recipe, it’s often best to stick with the unit provided or use a reliable converter to switch between grams and ounces accurately.
